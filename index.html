PolyInterview: A Multi-Agent Platform for Immersive and Personalized Job Interview Practice

Abstract

Realistic interview practice is costly and difficult to access. PolyInterview uses the job description and an optional CV to plan a mock interview. A lip-synced digital interviewer asks questions aloud, and a follow-up agent decides when to clarify an answer or probe the candidate’s reasoning.

Four assessment agents score 13 features of the candidate’s answers, speech, and nonverbal behavior. An aggregation agent combines the results in a report that uses the KSA framework to assess professional competency and STAR to guide feedback on behavioral answers.

Across 83 job positions, 377 registered users took part in 2,154 sessions that generated 11,124 questions. Ten experts rated question-plan relevance at 4.80/5 and feedback actionability at 4.70/5. Thirty users rated interview realism at 4.33/5 and interaction naturalness at 3.87/5.

System design

The planning agent matches role requirements to the candidate’s experience. The interviewer delivers each question through a digital avatar, while the follow-up agent checks the response for ambiguity, contradiction, or a detail worth exploring. Depending on the selected persona, each main question allows up to one, two, or three follow-ups.

Data and consent

PolyInterview is a free, noncommercial prototype for interview practice. Users consent to anonymized analysis of their interview records. The platform stores CVs and interview recordings on campus servers with encryption and account-based access controls. Users can delete their data at any time. Reports are for practice only and play no role in hiring decisions.
